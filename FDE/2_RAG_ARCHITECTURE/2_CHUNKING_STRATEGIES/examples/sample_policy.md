# Synthetic Lending Operations Guide

Teaching fixture for Markdown section chunking. This is fictional policy text, not legal or financial advice.

## Refund eligibility

A payment may be reviewed for a refund when the payment status is captured and the request is within the configured review window. A support agent should confirm the transaction reference before discussing next steps.

### Evidence to collect

- Synthetic payment reference, such as `pay_TEST_4421`
- Requested amount in USD
- Short reason for the request

## Identity verification

Verification status is checked using a synthetic customer reference such as `cust_TEST_001`. Never put government-issued identifiers into an example document or a log.

## Escalation

Escalate a request when its status is unknown, evidence conflicts, or a specialist review is required. Do not claim that a refund has been issued until a payment system confirms it.
